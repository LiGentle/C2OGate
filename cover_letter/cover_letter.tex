Dear Editors,

I am pleased to submit the manuscript
``C2OGate: Proof-Carrying Joint Performance Estimation for Two-Oracle Cost
Certification'' for consideration in \textit{Mathematical Programming
Computation}.

The manuscript presents an exact computational method for deciding whether a
candidate produced by a secondary oracle can be certified to reduce all-in
expensive-oracle cost relative to an unchanged baseline. C2OGate conditions on
observed first-order data, couples the two continuations on the same unknown
function, enumerates their joint stopping-time cells, and reduces every
cost-violating cell over smooth strongly convex functions to a semidefinite
program.

The main computational contribution is proof-carrying acceptance with explicit
failure semantics. Structural screeners or independently checked dual
certificates must exclude every cost-violating cell; a verified attainable
cell rejects, and any unresolved numerical status returns \emph{uncertified}.
The numerical optimizer is therefore outside the trusted acceptance boundary.
The paper proves the finite-horizon and exact dimension-free Gram-SDP
reductions, gives the fixed-dimension rank qualification, and shows that
separate marginal call bounds can be arbitrarily more conservative even within
one-dimensional quadratics.

The computational study includes complete structured and generic horizon-20
enumerations, exact acceptance of a nonquadratic instance, nine rational dual
certificates checked through 87 exact principal minors, 2,000 finite
transcript-consistent quadratic families, and a real-SPX cost instance reducing
7,840 baseline calls to 3,803 hybrid calls with an all-in ratio of 0.492. The
paper distinguishes exact acceptance, witnessed rejection, scaling evidence,
and accounting evidence; it does not promote inaccurate solver statuses to
proofs or claim universal acceleration.

This submission is designed for MPC's software and data review. The accompanying
artifact contains structured Python source, complete frozen inputs, canonical
hashes, rebuild scripts, unit and integrity tests, three standard-library exact
verifiers, and adversarial rehashed modifications. Exact proof replay is
separated from optional CVXPY--Clarabel experiment regeneration, so a Technical
Editor can inspect the acceptance evidence without trusting the search solver.
I believe this combination of a new stopping-cell computation, exact
certification, and evaluation-ready implementation fits MPC's emphasis on
significant developments in computational optimization.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and is
self-contained; it does not rely on an unpublished companion paper. I am the
sole author and approve the submission. Thank you for your consideration.
