Dear Editors,

I am pleased to submit the manuscript
``C2OGate: Proof-Carrying Joint Performance Estimation for Certified Branch
Decisions'' for consideration in \textit{Mathematical Programming Computation}.

The manuscript presents a proof-carrying computational architecture for one
certified branch decision.  An external proposal mechanism supplies a state
and an auditable error-and-cost contract; both alternatives then continue with
the same fixed-step gradient method.  C2OGate conditions on observed
first-order data, couples the two continuations on the same unknown function,
enumerates their joint stopping-time cells, and reduces every cost-violating
cell over smooth strongly convex functions to a semidefinite program.  The
paper does not claim to implement a complete secondary-oracle optimizer.

The main computational contribution is proof-carrying acceptance with explicit
failure semantics. Structural screeners or independently checked dual
certificates must exclude every cost-violating cell; a verified attainable
cell rejects, and any unresolved numerical status returns \emph{uncertified}.
The numerical optimizer is therefore outside the trusted acceptance boundary.
The paper proves the finite-horizon and exact dimension-free Gram-SDP
reductions, gives the fixed-dimension rank qualification, and shows that
separate marginal call bounds can be arbitrarily more conservative even within
one-dimensional quadratics.

The central computational demonstration has natural horizon $H=10$, nonzero
proposal-contract radius, 66 cost-violating generic Gram-SDP cells, Gram order
at most 24, and up to 534 inequalities per cell.  Rational dual recovery
succeeds for every cell in a recorded ordered threshold--regularizer search;
incomplete recovery would report certified progress and return
\emph{uncertified}.  A standard-library verifier reconstructs all affine
systems, stationarity equations, dual objectives, and slack matrices and
checks 1,584 exact positive $LDL^\top$ pivots.  Repeated Clarabel and SCS runs
report timing quantiles and peak memory, and a same-model PEPit comparison
separates generic PEP modeling from C2OGate's branch-disjunction,
fail-closed-aggregation, and exact-proof layers.  A separate exact instance
over the full infinite class $\mathcal F_{1/2,1}$ excludes all ten bad cells
and accepts with the joint stopping-time set while sharp independent marginals
reject.  It has a nonzero proposal radius and uses neither a shift identity nor
full Hessian revelation.  Rational recovery is explicitly presented as a
heuristic success-or-fail producer; the independent consumer supplies the
guarantee.  The generic horizon-20 study remains \emph{uncertified}, delimiting
the method as moderate-horizon exact infrastructure rather than promoting
solver statuses to proofs.

The manuscript also reports a fully charged cost ledger for this flagship
suite: frozen search and rational recovery, one complete independent verifier
replay, and the resulting prepaid-reuse thresholds are all charged against
the certificate's remaining cost budget.  This accounting does not use a
possibly rejected first online decision to finance its own proof.
An explicit repeated-PDE calculation additionally charges acquisition of the
curvature, distance, and residual envelope and gives a prepaid break-even
threshold on the frozen timing ledger when one forward--adjoint
gradient takes 60 seconds.

The paper also reports a contract-misspecification audit on 2,000 finite
families, including false-acceptance rates relative to the full class.  The
real-SPX result remains a constant-and-cost-pipeline stress test rather than a
short-transcript PEP acceptance.  Because the third-party snapshot cannot be
redistributed, the artifact includes a synthetic raw option panel that runs
the same filtering and data-to-rational-matrix reconstruction path.

This submission is designed for MPC's software and data review.  The
accompanying artifact contains structured Python source, complete frozen
derived proof inputs, canonical hashes, rebuild scripts, unit and integrity
tests, seven standard-library exact
verifiers, and adversarial rehashed modifications. Exact proof replay is
separated from optional CVXPY--Clarabel experiment regeneration, so a Technical
Editor can inspect the acceptance evidence without trusting the search solver.
I believe this combination of a new stopping-cell computation, exact
certification, and evaluation-ready implementation fits MPC's emphasis on
significant developments in computational optimization.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and is
self-contained; it does not rely on an unpublished companion paper. I am the
sole author and approve the submission. Thank you for your consideration.
